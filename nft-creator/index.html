Payment & payout
Non-custodial flow Buyer pays through the smart contract. Ticket revenue is intended to go directly to the organizer payout wallet, while ANGRY receives only the disclosed platform fee.
Platform-level setting. The creator cannot edit it. Exact live fee must be disclosed before deployment.
Network gas is separate from ticket price and changes with network conditions.
Payment flow Buyer pays ticket price + network gas → organizer receives ticket revenue minus disclosed ANGRY fee → NFT ticket mints directly to buyer.
